## Battery policy — UNACCEPTED, do not integrate

User explicitly wants70% cap on USB and Qi, external power carrying load at cap,
USB preferred when both present, no battery cycling/draw. Current deployed driver
retains Samsung store policy: charging off at70, buck off above70 to deliberately
discharge, recharge threshold60; power selection based on estimated input power.
This does NOT yet meet the requested external-power hold. No true battery-isolation
mode is exposed by the reviewed driver; cap/near-zero gauge readings do not prove
physical bypass or guaranteed zero battery draw under all loads.

`power/wip/12-stand-external-power.patch` and `check_stand_power.py` are explicitly
unaccepted. They add a per-battery cap latch, NOT_CHARGING status, central charging
request guard, swelling/temperature recovery handling and explicit wired allowlist.
The host C harness compiles but FAILS the protection case8: upstream CHARGE_DISABLE
rewrites CHARGING to CHARGING_OFF before the new guard, permitting protected
BUCK_OFF to be overwritten. Fix guard ordering and complete connected safety review
before any build/deploy. Factory-mode behavior also needs review. Do not merely
remove the assertion. Companion `power/wip/stand-policy-ui.patch` is unapplied.

Important safety findings: swelling protection triggers41°C/5°C while general
health may remain Good (thresholds50°C/0°C). Old cap DISCHARGING skips temperature,
voltage and SYSOVLO/VBAT_OVP handling. NOT_CHARGING alone causes temperature logic
to re-enable charging each monitor and swelling code to clear its state. Repeated
sec_bat_set_charge calls reset thermal counters. All these connected paths need
preservation, not just changing BUCK_OFF to CHARGING_OFF. Preserve USB negotiated
current limits; unknown cable type must not override Qi.

Separate vendor bug remains: mfc_get_chip_id ignores a failed read and classifies
an uninitialized byte. Patch10 avoids the unnecessary no-transition read but does
not fix firmware-update/verify callers; address all callers before changing helper
error semantics. Do not trust an IDT log immediately following an I2C failure.
