SECTION 1 · SKIP IF YOU ALREADY HAVE TWRP

Install TWRP

S9 / DOWNLOAD MODE

Writes only the RECOVERY partition (Odin protocol). Needs an OEM-unlocked S9 (SM-G960F, Exynos). Not for the Snapdragon SM-G9600.

  1. 01

    Power off. Hold Volume Down + Bixby, plug in USB, press Volume Up to enter Download Mode.

  2. 02

    Download twrp-3.7.0_9-0-starlte.img (or pick your own copy) below, then connect. The page checks its SHA-256 before writing.

  3. 03

    Right after the flash, do not let the phone boot normally (stock Android would restore the stock recovery). Hold Volume Down + Power until the screen goes black, then immediately hold Volume Up + Bixby + Power until the TWRP screen appears.

Put the phone in Download Mode, then connect.

Flash the postmarketOS server image

S9 / TWRP ADB

Writes samsung-starlte-root.img to SYSTEM, samsung-starlte-boot.img to VENDOR and boot.img to BOOT, then verifies each on the phone with SHA-256. Files stay in your browser and stream over USB.

  1. 01

    Boot TWRP (Volume Up + Bixby + Power). Do not mount system, vendor or data.

  2. 02

    Download the images (or pick the three files you already have), connect, and approve the ADB prompt on the phone if it appears.
